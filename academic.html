Our Academic Philosophy

At Mother Teresa E-Techno School, we follow a student-centered academic approach that promotes conceptual understanding, creativity, and critical thinking.

Our curriculum is designed to balance academics with co-curricular activities, ensuring holistic development of every child.

Curriculum Structure

Pre-Primary

Play-based learning focusing on language, numbers, and social skills.

Primary

Strong foundation in core subjects with activity-based learning.

Middle School

Conceptual clarity through experiments, projects, and assessments.

Mathematics

Logical reasoning, problem-solving, and analytical skills.

Languages

English, regional, and additional language proficiency.

Computer Education

Digital literacy, coding basics, and technology awareness.

Assessment & Evaluation

Continuous and comprehensive evaluation through periodic tests, assignments, projects, and examinations to monitor student progress.

Academic Support

Remedial classes, doubt-clearing sessions, and mentoring programs are provided to support every learner.
