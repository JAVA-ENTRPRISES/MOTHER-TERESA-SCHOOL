Welcome to Mother Teresa E-Techno School

Mother Teresa E-Techno School is committed to providing quality education in a disciplined, safe, and nurturing environment. Our institution focuses on academic excellence along with character building and moral values.

With experienced faculty, modern teaching methodologies, and well-equipped infrastructure, we ensure that every child receives holistic development.

We aim to inspire curiosity, creativity, and confidence in our students, preparing them for future challenges.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution that nurtures knowledge, values, and skills to create responsible global citizens.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality education through innovative teaching, experienced faculty, and a student-friendly environment that encourages excellence.

Message from the Principal

Our goal is to create a strong foundation for students by combining academic rigor with ethical values. We believe every child has unique potential, and we strive to bring out the best in each one.
